# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Aptitude App values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

## 1. Information We Collect
We may collect the following information:
- Account and profile information you provide when using the app
- Learning activity, progress, and quiz performance data
- Coins, rewards, and unlocked topic data
- Device information such as operating system version and app version
- Usage data for improving app performance and user experience

## 2. How We Use Information
We use the information to:
- Provide and improve the learning experience
- Track progress, rewards, and achievements
- Maintain security and app reliability
- Develop better features and content
- Diagnose issues and improve overall performance

## 3. Local Storage
The app may store some information locally on your device to remember user progress, coin balance, daily rewards, and unlocked topics. This data is used to enhance usability and experience.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We may use analytics, crash reporting, or hosting tools from third parties. These services may collect limited technical information subject to their privacy policies.

## 5. Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect personal data, but no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute protection against all risks.

## 6. Your Choices
You may choose not to provide some information, but this may limit certain features of the app.

## 7. Children’s Privacy
This app is intended for general users and should not be used by children without proper parental consent or supervision, as required by local law.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after updates means you accept the revised policy.

## 9. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the app owner through the official project repository or support channel.
